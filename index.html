How to play

Fill a row edge to edge and it clears. Clear several at once, chain clears back to back, and twist the T-piece into tight gaps for big points.

Touch

Drag sidewaysMove. The piece follows your finger, anywhere on screen.
TapRotate. Right half turns clockwise, left half counter-clockwise.
Drag downSoft drop
Flick downHard drop
Flick upHold (or tap the Hold box)

Keyboard

← →Move
↓Soft drop
SpaceHard drop
↑ / XRotate clockwise
Z / CtrlRotate counter-clockwise
C / ShiftHold
Esc / PPause

Modes

Zen never ends. If the stack reaches the top, the blocks tumble away and you keep your score. Pause and choose Finish session to log it.

Classic speeds up every 10 lines. Top out and the run is over.

Install on iPhone

Open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It launches full-screen and plays offline.
